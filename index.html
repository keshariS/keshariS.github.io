About

Hardware × Software
Researcher & Engineer

Hi, I'm Shuvam — a Systems Integration Engineer at Tesla working on energy storage products like Powerwall 3, and an independent researcher in Generative AI.

My unusual path across both hardware and software was shaped by two visionaries: Steve Jobs, who believed those serious about software must make their own hardware — and Elon Musk, whose sense of urgency birthed both Tesla and SpaceX. I came to firmly believe that most meaningful problems demand engineering solutions that bridge both worlds.

I hold a Masters degree (in Electrical and Computer Engineering) from UT Austin and a Bachelors degree (major in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science) from IIT Kharagpur — one of India's top engineering institutions.

I aspire to build products that realize a greener tomorrow through sustainable transportation, autonomous vehicles, and intelligent systems. I dream of humanity reaching a Type II Kardashev civilization — where a Matrioshka brain may one day become a reality.

"Those who are serious about their software, make their own hardware." — Steve Jobs
